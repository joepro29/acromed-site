Recent Projects

A selection of recent equipment supply and installation projects completed by our team

ACROMED Healthcare Technologies has completed medical equipment supply and installation projects for a wide range of healthcare facilities, from small rural clinics to large referral hospitals. Recent projects include the installation of digital X-Ray systems and patient monitors in Kampala, the supply and commissioning of laboratory analysers for a regional diagnostic centre in Jinja, and the deployment of surgical theatre equipment at a private hospital in Gulu. Each project involved end-to-end management including needs assessment, equipment sourcing, delivery coordination, on-site installation by our biomedical engineers, and hands-on clinical training for the facility's staff. We are proud of the trust our clients place in us and remain committed to delivering the same high standard of service on every project, regardless of size or location.
