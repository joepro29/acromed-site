Contact Us

We're here to help — reach out for quotations, service requests, or product enquiries

Whether you need a quotation, a service visit, or information about a product, our team is ready to help — and we respond to every enquiry within 24 hours on business days. For urgent technical support, call us directly. For quotations, use the form or email so you can attach specifications.

How we handle your enquiry

  • Within minutes — your form submission reaches our team as an email notification.
  • Within 2 hours (business hours) — we review and respond to your message.
  • Same day or next business day — quotations with pricing and specifications.
  • Priority for critical equipment — patient monitors, ventilators, and sterilisation gear are triaged first.
  • Service requests — our coordinator calls to confirm details and schedule an engineer visit.

Our Service Areas

We serve healthcare facilities across Uganda and East Africa

Based in Kampala, our team travels nationwide and across the region. We have completed installations in all Kampala divisions, Wakiso, Entebbe, Jinja, Gulu, Lira, Mbale, Tororo, Mbarara, Masaka, Fort Portal, Kabale, Kasese, Arua, and many more towns — plus Nairobi and Mombasa (Kenya), Dar es Salaam and Arusha (Tanzania), and Juba (South Sudan).

Equipment delivery

  • Kampala: delivered within 2–3 business days of order confirmation.
  • Upcountry: 5–7 business days, with fragile medical cargo handled by specialist logistics partners.
  • International (East Africa): coordinated case by case, depending on border customs clearance.
  • Tracking provided for all deliveries, scheduled around your team's availability.
  • Bulk orders: staged deliveries can be aligned with your construction or renovation schedule.
