About ACROMED

Elevating Healthcare Standards Across East Africa

Who We Are

ACROMED Healthcare Technologies Limited is a Ugandan-registered company (Certificate of Incorporation No. 80034002521141) specialising in the supply, installation, and maintenance of medical equipment and consumables. We provide end-to-end solutions from procurement through installation to after-sales support, serving hospitals, clinics, diagnostic centres, and maternity facilities across Uganda and East Africa.

Founded in 2021, ACROMED was established to close a gap in Uganda's healthcare technology market — reliable, affordable equipment backed by genuine after-sales support. Having spent years in clinical engineering and procurement across East Africa, our founding team saw how poor equipment and missing service contracts disrupted patient care. That shaped our core philosophy: every product we supply must be matched by professional installation, thorough training, and responsive maintenance.

With 4+ years of experience, we partner with Mindray, Contec, Shenzhen Medical, Locmedt, Zeiss, and DLAB — brands chosen for their East African track record, spare-part availability, and commitment to training and documentation.

Our History & Team

Founded to bridge the gap between international medical device manufacturers and East African healthcare facilities, ACROMED has grown from a small supply company into a comprehensive healthcare technology partner serving hospitals and clinics across multiple countries.

Our team

  • Senior biomedical engineer — 10+ years installing and calibrating medical devices across Kampala, Nairobi, and Dar es Salaam.
  • Procurement specialist — manages manufacturer relationships across China, Europe, and the US, ensuring regulatory and quality compliance.
  • Service & maintenance lead — coordinates field technicians for installations, preventive maintenance, and emergency repairs nationwide.
  • Continuous training — our engineers attend manufacturer-led programmes every year on new product lines, software, and advanced troubleshooting.

Our certifications

  • Certificate of Incorporation — ACROMED Healthcare Technologies Limited, legally registered in Uganda.
  • EGP Uganda Certification — approved supplier on the Government e-Procurement platform for public-sector facilities.
  • Business License — authorised to trade in medical equipment and supplies, renewed annually with URSB.
  • Certificates are provided to clients on request for tenders and due diligence.
