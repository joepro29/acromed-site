Our Products

High-quality medical equipment from trusted global manufacturers

Product Categories

We supply a comprehensive range of medical equipment for hospitals, clinics, and laboratories across East Africa.

ACROMED partners with trusted international manufacturers to bring reliable, high-value medical devices to healthcare facilities in Uganda and East Africa — from diagnostic imaging and patient monitoring to laboratory and surgical equipment, each backed by full installation, training, and after-sales support.

Our quality assurance process

  • Pre-shipment inspection at the manufacturer's facility verifies specifications, functionality, and packaging integrity.
  • Incoming checks in Kampala — visual inspection for transit damage plus functional testing of key parameters.
  • Documentation verified — user manuals, warranty cards, and certificates of conformity confirmed on every delivery.
  • Climate-controlled storage protects equipment from humidity and temperature damage before delivery to your facility.

Equipment selection guide

Recommendations based on your facility's patient volume, infrastructure, and budget:

  • Small clinic (up to 50 patients/day): vital signs monitor, portable ultrasound, and an entry-level haematology analyser.
  • District hospital (50–150 beds): add a digital X-Ray system, multi-parameter ICU monitor, and semi-automated chemistry analyser.
  • Referral & large private facilities: advanced imaging, central monitoring stations, automated laboratory lines, and surgical theatre suites.
  • Site visit included: our team assesses power stability, space, network, and staffing before recommending equipment that fits your operations.

Warranty & after-sales coverage

  • 12-month warranty standard on all equipment — repair or replacement of defective components at your facility.
  • Extended 24 or 36-month warranties available on patient monitors, ultrasound systems, and lab analysers.
  • Warranty certificate delivered with every order detailing coverage, exclusions, and how to request service.
  • Renewable maintenance contracts keep equipment reliable long after purchase. See our Services page.
