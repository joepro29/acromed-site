Our Services

Complete lifecycle support for your medical equipment

What We Offer

From procurement to maintenance, we handle every stage of your medical equipment journey.

ACROMED provides complete lifecycle support for your medical equipment — sourcing, installation, training, and maintenance — delivered by experienced biomedical engineers who work closely with your hospital, clinical, and procurement teams.

Our installation process — step by step

  1. Site assessment visit — we evaluate power quality, space, network, lighting, and staffing, and flag any prep work needed.
  2. Delivery & pre-installation inspection — equipment is unpacked and checked so all accessories, cables, and documents are present and intact.
  3. Physical installation — mounting, electrical connection, network integration, and mechanical assembly as required.
  4. Calibration & performance verification — test samples and test patients confirm accurate results against manufacturer specs.
  5. Hands-on clinical training — two to four hours per device covering daily operation, alarms, cleaning, and troubleshooting.
  6. Documentation & handover — quick-reference cards, manuals, warranty certificates, and service contacts.
  7. 48-hour follow-up call — we confirm everything works and answer any questions that arise in the first days.

Service response times

  • Premium plan: on-site response within 24 hours in Kampala, 48 hours upcountry.
  • Standard plan: priority scheduling with on-site response within 72 hours.
  • Basic plan: phone and remote support during business hours, on-site within 5 business days.
  • Engineers carry spare parts for major lines (Mindray, Contec, Locmedt) to reduce follow-up visits.

Training — practical, hands-on, at your facility

  • Basic operation — powering on, navigating menus, running tests/scans, and interpreting results.
  • Alarm interpretation — what each alarm means and how your staff should respond.
  • Cleaning & disinfection — device-specific protocols following manufacturer and infection-control guidance.
  • Basic troubleshooting — common errors, sensor and consumable changes, and when to call for service.
  • Train-the-trainer for larger facilities, plus refresher sessions included in maintenance contracts.

How We Work

Our service delivery follows a structured process to ensure quality and reliability at every stage.

  1. Needs assessment — we review your requirements, infrastructure, and budget.
  2. Detailed proposal — equipment specs, pricing, timelines, installation plan, and service options.
  3. Logistics — importation, customs clearance, and delivery coordination handled end to end.
  4. Installation & training — certified engineers install, test, and train your staff on site.
  5. Ongoing support — maintenance and support programmes keep equipment performing at its best.
